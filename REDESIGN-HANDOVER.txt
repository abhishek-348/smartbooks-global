SMARTBOOKS GLOBAL — US BUSINESS WEBSITE REDESIGN
Prepared 16 October 2026

REVIEW STATUS
A preview redesign of the existing SmartBooks Global service website.
Production website is unchanged until this branch is approved for release.

POSITIONING AND CONTENT
US business-owner focus; bookkeeping, reporting and advisory described in plain English.
Distinct deep teal, warm white and mint visual system, responsive layouts and consistent typography.
Approved founder portrait used on the homepage and About page.
15 core website pages, including six service pages with scope, audience, expected outputs, starting information and FAQs.
Starting USD prices retain the current public figures: Starter $299, Growth $599, CFO Advisory $999 per month. The proposal sets final scope and fees. Unlimited transaction promises and unsupported turnaround guarantees are removed.
No fabricated customer counts, ratings, testimonials, US offices, CPA licences or security certifications.
Existing published QuickBooks and Intuit credentials are retained; the founder is described as a Chartered Accountant in India, not a US CPA.
Payroll coordination and tax-ready bookkeeping have clear boundaries and coordination with the appropriate providers or tax professionals.

PAGES AND TOOLS
Home, Services, six dedicated service pages, Who We Help, Pricing, About, Contact, Resources, Privacy and Service Information.
Illustrative profit-and-loss/cash-flow reporting panel, with consistent sample data and keyboard-accessible tabs. It is explicitly not a real client report or live dashboard.
Service-specific preparation checklist, current-book-state selection, checkboxes and text download.
Cash runway and break-even planning tools. Browser only; no calculator inputs are submitted.
Service and plan context carried into the enquiry journey; direct email preparation option.
Existing Tally enquiry form NpM2Nl retained, with native dynamic-height embed support and an external fallback. No real enquiry was submitted during testing.
SEO titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, Organization schema, favicon, robots file and sitemap.
No analytics, CRM, appointment scheduler, automatic email or service checkout has been added.

EXISTING PRODUCT AREA
Founder Clarity pages, checkout/download API, commerce/fulfillment libraries, tests, dependencies, storage, credentials and payment configuration are unchanged.
The build continues to copy the existing Founder Clarity area to public output.
Live read-only checkout availability was verified as {"available":false} while preparing this preview.
No paid product bundle is included in public assets. No payment or launch flag has been enabled.
