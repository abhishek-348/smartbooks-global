PRIVACY

Your information.
Handled with purpose.

Please review this information before submitting an enquiry.

Enquiries

Information you choose to share is used to understand and respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential engagement. Provide only what is needed for the initial conversation.

Third-party forms and communications

The enquiry form is hosted by Tally. Your information is handled through that provider and our business communications. External services have their own privacy terms. Email links open your mail application.

Tools and website services

Planning calculator and checklist inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to SmartBooks Global by these tools. The site is hosted on Vercel and uses Google Fonts; requests to these services are subject to their own practices. This redesign does not add analytics or advertising trackers.

Engagement information

Document-sharing arrangements and access permissions are agreed during onboarding. Do not provide passwords, bank records, identity documents, or other sensitive information through the general enquiry form.

Questions or requests

Contact support@smartbooksglobal.com about information you have shared or any related request.
