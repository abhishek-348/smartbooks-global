Questions about scope and pricing.

Are these final fixed fees?+

These are starting monthly prices in US dollars. Your written proposal confirms the applicable fee and the included scope after reviewing your requirements.

Does CFO Advisory include unlimited bookkeeping?+

No automatic unlimited scope is assumed. Bookkeeping volume, reporting responsibilities, meeting cadence, and advisory deliverables are agreed in the proposal.

Can I start with cleanup only?+

Yes. A standalone setup, catch-up, or cleanup engagement can be scoped before discussing ongoing monthly support.

Do I need to pay through the website?+

No. The website starts an enquiry. Engagement and payment arrangements are agreed separately.
