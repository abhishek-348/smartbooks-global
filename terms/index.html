Scope and engagement

Service descriptions explain areas of support. A professional relationship begins with an agreed engagement, including scope, fees, responsibilities, deliverables, and timing.

Starting prices

Published monthly amounts are starting prices in US dollars. Final fees depend on the business, account and transaction volumes, condition of records, reporting requirements, and agreed level of support. Historical cleanup, setup, software and provider charges, and tax filings are separately scoped where applicable.

Professional responsibilities

SmartBooks Global provides bookkeeping, accounting support, and financial advisory. No US CPA licensure, audit opinion, tax representation, investment advice, or legal advice is implied by the website. Any specialist or jurisdiction-specific work requires the appropriate separately agreed scope and professional.

Resources

Checklists, planning tools, and sample reports illustrate concepts and help with preparation. They are not a financial forecast or professional recommendation for your circumstances. Example figures are clearly illustrative.

Separate digital resources

Founder Clarity OS is a separate product area. Its availability and terms are described on those pages. This service enquiry website does not collect payments or confirm appointments.
