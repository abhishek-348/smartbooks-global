A few common questions

How do you set the fee?+

We consider the number of accounts, transaction volumes, entities, condition of records, reporting needs, and agreed service cadence.

Can you work with my existing tax professional?+

Yes. Bookkeeping and reporting can be coordinated with your CPA or tax preparer, with your permission and clearly agreed responsibilities.

How will information be shared?+

We agree the appropriate access permissions and document-sharing process during onboarding. Please do not send passwords or sensitive records through a general enquiry.
