IS THIS FOR YOU?

A good fit when…

Owners who want fewer last-minute document requests and better coordination between bookkeeping and their tax professional.

What we can handle

  • Year-end financial statement preparation support
  • Reconciled balance sheet schedules
  • Coordination on bookkeeping questions from your CPA
  • Organization of agreed year-end records

What you can expect

An agreed year-end bookkeeping package and coordination on related questions. Tax filing or representation is separately scoped with the appropriate professional.

Clear scope before we start.

We review the current state of your books, agree the periods and deliverables, and identify what is included. Timing and fees follow that review.

A few common questions

How do you set the fee?+

We consider the number of accounts, transaction volumes, entities, condition of records, reporting needs, and agreed service cadence.

Can you work with my existing tax professional?+

Yes. Bookkeeping and reporting can be coordinated with your CPA or tax preparer, with your permission and clearly agreed responsibilities.

How will information be shared?+

We agree the appropriate access permissions and document-sharing process during onboarding. Please do not send passwords or sensitive records through a general enquiry.
