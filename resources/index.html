How long does your cash last?

Cash runway at a constant monthly net outflow.

Available cash ÷ monthly net outflow. Excludes new funding and changes in collections or spending.

What revenue covers your costs?

Monthly break-even at your contribution margin.

Fixed costs ÷ contribution margin. Contribution margin is revenue less variable costs, as a percentage of revenue.
UNDERSTAND / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Do I need QuickBooks Online already?+

No. We can discuss setup or migration as a separate part of the engagement. Access, subscriptions, and any migration work are confirmed in scope.

Can you help with books that are behind?+

Yes. We begin with the periods involved and available records, then scope the catch-up or cleanup required.

Do you replace my CPA?+

Bookkeeping, financial reporting, and advisory are distinct from tax filing and licensed CPA services. We can coordinate with your CPA or tax preparer with your permission.

When will I receive my monthly reports?+

The close process and reporting cadence are agreed during onboarding, including the information and approvals needed from you.

Will I be given a fixed monthly fee?+

After reviewing your needs, the written proposal confirms the fee and scope. Changes in volume or complexity are discussed rather than assumed.

Can you work with a US-based business remotely?+

Yes. We coordinate online meetings, access, and document sharing. Communication windows and meeting times are agreed for the engagement.

How do I share bank records or passwords?+

Do not send passwords or sensitive financial records through a general enquiry. We agree appropriate user access and document sharing during onboarding.
